How I work

Three steps. You always know what's happening and where we are.

  1. 01

    We talk

    You tell me what you need and what's broken. I ask questions, check what's possible and give you a fixed quote. No jargon, no surprises.

  2. 02

    I build it

    I design the structure, use AI to write the code and keep the decisions in my hands. You see progress while it's being built.
